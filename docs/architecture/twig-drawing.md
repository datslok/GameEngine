# Twig drawing

Covers: `src/scene/twig_set`, `src/scene/twig_far_levels`, `src/scene/foliage_shading`, `src/scene/foliage_shared.h`, `assets/shaders/foliage_shading.glsl`, `src/render/gpu/twig_records`, `src/render/gpu/twig_drawing_shared.h`, `src/render/vulkan/vulkan_twig_drawing`, `assets/shaders/twig_drawing.glsl`, `assets/shaders/twig_record.glsl`, `assets/shaders/twig_pass.glsl`, `assets/shaders/twig_sources.comp`, `assets/shaders/twig_cull.comp`

A plant's twigs drawn as instances of a few prototype meshes, culled a brick, then a twig, before their clusters (Nanite's assemblies; `forest-twigs.md` for the forest's).

- **What a game hands over** (`scene/twig_set`, `TwigSet`): the prototypes (drawn-only meshes with given cluster levels), each twig (`TwigInstance`: foot, frame, scale, prototype, lean) and the bricks (`TwigBrick`, `twigBrickSize` 64). `makeTwigSet` splits the feet's box along its longest side at whole bricks until each part is one brick (`medianSplitOrder`, `math/median_split.h`, a median split: compact bricks, all full but the last; a Morton order's runs left one brick 12.6 m across in a 14 m crown), each brick's sphere its farthest twig's foot plus that twig's reach (`meshReach` of its prototype, times its scale).
- **Records** (`render/gpu/twig_records`): `GpuTwig` (64 B: the 3 x 4 matrix from prototype to plant frame, lean, prototype; its bone's binding a word beside it, `packTwigBinding`, scene binding 49: the twig moves whole by its bone's pose, `wind-bones.md`), `GpuTwigBrick` (32 B), `GpuTwigLevel` (32 B a prototype's level: its group's sphere and error, its clusters' run in the pool; `packTwigLevels` refuses clusters that are not a chain).
- **The twig pass's maths** (`twig_drawing.glsl`, mirrored in `render/gpu/twig_records`; 3b-iii-b1): `twigInstance` (the part's instance with the twig's matrix after it, the twig's turn after its normals, its scale on the part's); `twigLevelDrawn` and `twigLevel` (the cluster cut on whole levels: the level whose own error, the level before's group's, fits and whose group's does not, so the twig pass picks exactly the clusters `meshClusterInCut` keeps); `twigBrickKept` and `twigKept` (kept whole where the part is seen whole, as `meshClusterSeenWhole`; else, posed by its tree's bones, its posed sphere, `twigBrickPose`, `twigPose`, [wind-bones.md](wind-bones.md); else the brick's sphere placed by the part, or the prototype's coarsest group's placed by the twig, in the frustum). `twigDrawsOnCpu` runs the pass's steps for a part (`TwigTables`, `TwigDraw`). Twig entries from `TWIG_INSTANCE_BASE`; a level at most `TWIG_MOST_LEVEL_CLUSTERS` clusters, a prototype at most `TWIG_MOST_LEVELS` levels (`twig_drawing_shared.h`).
- **Twig parts** (3b-iii-b2a): a part carrying a twig set (`RenderPart::twigs`, `DrawItem::twigs`, `CULL_TWIGS`) is drawn only by views drawing twigs (`LevelView::twigs`, `CULL_LEVELS_TWIGS`: the camera drawing mesh clusters with Passes "Twigs" on, off until the twig pass draws), and what it replaces (`ModelRenderer::twigFallback`, `CULL_TWIG_FALLBACK`) only by the others (`cameraTakesTwigs` in `cameraCandidateFlags`); shadows take the fallback either way. In the placed table a twig part's groups draw nothing and its `unused[0]` names its set in `PlacedTable::twigSets`. Its mesh only stands for it (bounds, levels).
- **Drawn** (3b-iii-b2b, `render/vulkan/vulkan_twig_drawing`, Passes "Twigs", on by default: the user, Nov 22 2026): after [PLACED], `twig_sources` (one thread: the camera's placed entries of twig parts, each with its set) and `twig_cull` (a workgroup a source's brick: `twigBrickKept`, `twigKept`, the level walk, an atomic append into the region of the level's cluster count, `GpuTwigEntry`), GPU timer TWIGS. The cluster passes take regions as groups from `TWIG_GROUP_BASE` in the twig parts' style (`PlacedTable::twigStyle`, bindings 18-20); the mesh shaders' `drawnObject` returns the part's instance and the twig's matrix, which `shadeVertex` applies to the vertex first (scene bindings 46-47). Prototype levels come from the pool's records (`GpuMeshCluster::level`). Room grows from the counts read back. `cluster-compare` does not know twigs yet.

- **F4 `TWIGS`** (5-0: where the twig triangles come from): `twig_cull` counts in shared memory and adds once a workgroup (`TWIG_TALLY_*` words after the regions' room, copied back with the counts, `TwigTally`, `readTwigTally`): twigs tested (every twig of every source's bricks), kept, kept moving (posed by their bones, or kept whole as their part sways unbound), and each level's twigs and triangles (`GpuTwigLevel::triangleCount`). `twigDrawsOnCpu` adds the same tally. Line: `TWIGS kept/tested  SWAY n  LEVELS a b ...  TRIS aK bK ...`, up to the coarsest level drawn.
- **Leaf look** (3b-iii-c, `scene/foliage_shading`, `foliage_shading.glsl`): in `shadeVertex`, a twig's normal is `blendedFoliageNormal` of its own and `bentFoliageNormal` (the cards' 30% facing plus the twig's lean, never under 0.1 to the facing; the forest's cards use the same function) by Materials "Leaf normal blend" (`MaterialSettings::leafNormalBlend`, 1: the cards' look; `GpuFrameData::leafOptions.x`). Each leaf's tone: a prototype numbers its leaves in the texture coordinate's whole part (`numberedLeafU`; the surface sampler repeats), the vertex shader swaps it for `leafToneSeed(plant pick bits, twig, leaf)` (`tonedLeafU`, 64 seeds: 8 tones 0.85-1.1 x 8 warmths -6..6%, the cards' ranges, `foliage_shared.h`), and a material with `leafTones` (`MATERIAL_LEAF_TONES`) multiplies its colour by `leafToneScale` (the factors on sRGB, to linear by gamma 2.2). A leaf keeps its number at every level, so its tone never pops.

## Far levels above the twigs (3e)

Bricks take over from their twigs at `twigBrickLevel` (2), as their own levels (Nanite's assemblies: a part merged once it is one cluster). Plan: `docs/research/LEAFGEOMETRY.md`, Far levels above the twigs. Built on the CPU (3e-i), records and their vertices for the GPU (3e-ii), every set's tables packed and each brick's choice made on the CPU twin (3e-iii-a), drawn (3e-iii-b).

- **What a prototype hands over** (`TwigFarLeaves`): its level-2 leaves (`TwigLeafPlace`: foot, frame, side, shape area, reach, atlas cell, number), that level's growth and error.
- **Records** (`FarLeaf`, plant frame): a leaf carried by its twig (`farLeafOf`: foot and frame turned and placed, side times the twig's scale and the level's growth), its cell, number and twig (the twig's binding, lean and tone serve it). A one-row leaf is 5 vertices and 4 triangles, so a cluster holds `farLeavesPerCluster` (12, by the 64-vertex limit).
- **The tree** (`makeTwigFarLevels`, `TwigSet::far`, `TwigFarLevels`): nodes 0 to bricks - 1 are the bricks; two nodes merge as the bricks' median split paired them (`twigBricksFirstHalf`), each merge after its children, the root last (`TwigFarNode`: sphere, bricks' run, levels' run, children, parent).
- **Levels** (`TwigFarLevel`): every leaf of the set ranked once (`prunedOrder` over brick, twig and leaf order), a level of density k keeping one leaf in 2^k, so each level is a subset of every finer one, across merges too. A brick's level 0 is its twigs' level-2 leaves exactly; each level after keeps half, its survivors grown by sqrt(the node's full area over the kept area), until one cluster is left; a merged node starts past both children's last density, again until one cluster. Each level its own run of grown records (twice the finest's records; sharing them would save about 7 MB and add a growth a level), in its leaves' feet's median split order (`medianSplitOrder` by `farLeavesPerCluster`), so each run of 12, a cluster when packed, holds neighbours: on the test crown a level-0 cluster's sphere 1.24 m, not 2.41 m in the twigs' order (3e-v-a).
- **Errors** (plant frame), each at the error percentile (`nearestRank`; Twigs "Far error percentile", 50, at start-up; 100 is the worst leaf): a brick's level 0 its leaves' twig errors (level 2's times the twig's scale, once a leaf); then its grown leaves' strays past their finest sides (side minus finest side), never less than the level before's or the children's. At 100 a brick waited for its largest leaf, so it stayed at level 0 where most of its twigs had gone on (3e-iv).
- **Shapes** (`FarLeafShapes`, kept in `TwigFarLevels::shapes`; the forest's `twigFarLeafShapes`): each atlas cell's leaf traced with one row (base, tip, row height, half widths) and texture rectangle, and the fold and curl every leaf shares. A record names its cell.
- **For the GPU** (`render/gpu/twig_records`, mirrored by `twig_record.glsl`): `GpuFarLeaf` (28 B: foot; frame as the quaternion taking x, y, z to across, towardsTip, facing, four snorm16, `Quat::fromBasis`; side as a half float, cell and number a byte each; twig), `GpuFarLeafShape` (48 B, the fold as its tangent). `farLeafVertex` makes a vertex as `appendModelledLeaf` makes a one-row leaf's (`FAR_LEAF_VERTICES` 5: base, left edge, midrib, right edge, tip; every corner placed so a vertex's normal sums its triangles' facings), its texture coordinate numbered by the leaf, its tangent across; `farLeafTriangle` the 4 triangles (`FAR_LEAF_TRIANGLES`, `scene/twig_far_levels_shared.h`). Rounding: snorm16 about 3e-5 of a turn, half floats 5e-4 of a side, under 0.1 mm on a 10 cm leaf.
- **Spheres:** a node's holds its leaves at every level (foot plus side times reach) and its children's spheres; rest only (posed bounds are 3e-iii).
- **Packed for the pass** (`packTwigSets`, used by `VulkanTwigDrawing::prepare`): every set's bricks, twigs, bindings, far nodes (`GpuTwigFarNode`, 32 B: sphere, levels' run, first brick, parent), far levels (`GpuTwigFarLevel`, 16 B: leaves' run, error), far leaves and cells' shapes in one table each, every run moved past the sets before; a far leaf's twig becomes the table's number, since it names the twig's binding, lean and tone. A set's record (`GpuTwigSet`): its bricks' run, its first node (brick k's node is first + k; `TWIG_FAR_NO_NODE` without far levels), its first shape (sets with the same shapes share one run).
- **One choice a brick** (3e-iii-a, `twigDrawsOnCpu`; the cluster cut's rule on the far tree): a node fits where its finest level's error on its sphere, placed by the part, fits the pixel error (`twigFarLevelFits`). A parent's sphere holds its children's and its errors are never less, so the nodes that fit run from a brick up its chain. A brick whose own node does not fit draws its twigs; else it stands for the highest node up its chain that fits (`twigFarNodeDrawn`), the same node for every brick under it, drawn once by its first brick where its sphere is kept (`twigFarNodeKept`), at its coarsest level that fits (`twigFarLevel`). Twigs of a set with far levels stop at `TWIG_BRICK_LEVEL`. Where a brick's node fits, the twigs holding at least the error percentile of its leaves would draw that level or coarser by themselves (at 100 every twig: the bricks take over from exactly the leaves the twigs showed; below, its largest twigs' leaves may shrink to their level-2 forms; tested on the forest's twigs). GLSL: `twigFarLevelFits`, `twigFarNodeKept`, `twigSpherePose` in `twig_drawing.glsl`; `twig_cull` makes the choice (its first thread walks the chain), the node kept by its posed sphere (`twigSpherePose`: grown by the tree's move, as a brick's).
- **Far leaf clusters** (3e-iii-b, `GpuTwigFarCluster`, 32 B): each far level's leaves cut in order into clusters of `farLeavesPerCluster` (12) at packing, each with the sphere of its leaves' vertices as `farLeafVertex` makes them (the box's middle, out to the farthest); a level names its clusters' run. The chosen level's clusters go one an entry into the far region (`TWIG_FAR_REGION`, after the four twig regions; `TWIG_REGIONS` counts in `TWIG_REGION_WORDS`), the entry's first cluster the far cluster's number with `TWIG_FAR_CLUSTER_BIT`.
- **Through the cluster passes:** the far region is a group of one cluster an entry; a far pair is tested by its stand-in (`twigFarMeshCluster`: its sphere, always in the cut, never culled by facing, no bones), posed as its part's tree (`twigSpherePose`, when the part is bound), so frustum and both occlusion lists cull it as any cluster (`meshClusterOfPair` on both sides; `MeshClusterDrawn::farCluster`, `partBound`). Pass binding `MESH_CLUSTER_FAR_BINDING`; scene bindings 54-56 (`SCENE_FAR_*`: clusters, leaves, shapes).
- **F4 `FAR`** (3e-iii-c; `TwigTally`, `TWIG_TALLY_FAR_*`): bricks handing over to a far node, far nodes drawn (those merged from bricks apart), their levels' clusters and thousands of triangles (before the cluster passes' culling), each counted once a workgroup by its first thread. Line: `FAR BRICKS b  NODES n (m MERGED)  CLUSTERS c  TRIS tK`, shown once a brick hands over. With far leaf occlusion, `  LEAVES HIDDEN h/t (LATE h/t)`: the far leaves the main pass's mesh shaders hid of those they tested, then the late prepass's (`Collected::leafTally`: four words after both lists' totals, `MESH_CLUSTER_LEAF_TALLY_*`, cleared at cull, two atomics a workgroup, copied back after the main pass).
- **Passes "Far leaves"** (`RenderSettings::farLeaves`, `engine.render.passes.farLeaves`, on): off, no brick hands over and twigs go to every level, as before 3e (a push constant of the twig pass), for comparing.
- **Drawn** (`mesh_cluster_draw.glsl`'s `drawFarCluster`): the mesh shader's far branch makes each leaf's pose once (`farLeafPose`: its own twig's rigid pose, the twig named in `TwigPose::farTwig`), then each vertex with `farLeafVertex` through `shadeVertex` as its part's twig vertex with no matrix (`drawnObject`), taking its twig's tone and lean; its triangles each leaf's `farLeafTriangle`s.
- **Each far leaf occluded** (3e-v-b): a leaf (about 0.1 m) hides far more often than its cluster's sphere (1.24 m at level 0), so the mesh shader tests each one. `shadeVertex` returns the vertex measured from the camera (a mesh shader may not read its outputs); its clip position goes to shared memory; one thread a leaf takes its five vertices' screen box (`farLeafScreenBox`: their hull's box, the nearest depth `MESH_CLUSTER_LEAF_DEPTH_MARGIN` nearer) and tests it against a pyramid (`meshClusterBoxHiddenIn`, `mesh_cluster_pyramid.glsl`); a hidden leaf's 4 triangles get `gl_CullPrimitiveEXT`, dropped before rasterising. Its vertices are still placed (their count is set first). Cost: one pyramid read a leaf, only in far clusters kept.
- **Which pyramid** (the mesh shaders' third push constant, `PassDraws::farLeafTest`, `MESH_CLUSTER_LEAF_TEST_*`): none in the early prepass (last frame's depth could hide a leaf seen now, and nothing would draw it again); this frame's early list's in the late prepass; the whole frame's (the history, built before the main pass) in the main pass, which holds the leaf's own depth, hence the margin (1e-4 of the depth, 1 cm at 100 m), so a leaf seen anywhere is never culled and leaves no hole. Scene bindings 57-58 (`SCENE_LEAF_PYRAMID_*`); the pyramids are read in `ImageState::MeshComputeRead`. Passes "Far leaf occlusion" (`RenderSettings::farLeafOcclusion`, `engine.render.passes.farLeafOcclusion`, on; needs mesh cluster occlusion), off for comparing.
- **Cost:** built once a variant at start-up, about 48 ms for the 11 (release, home PC); memory grows with the variants' leaves (625K records, about 14 MB at 24 B), not with the trees planted.

## Fading into the card (3g)

A crown hands over to its impostor card across the camera's fade band ([level-of-detail.md](level-of-detail.md), Level fades): the trees' leaf mesh and card fade (`fadesLevels`, set in `forest_game.cpp`; the twigs' part takes the leaves' options), so the card dithers in. The twigs are opaque, with no prepass fragment shader to discard, so they shrink away instead (SpeedTree's and the old leaf cards' way, `docs/research/LOD.md`):

- **Rank** (`twigFadeRank`, `twig_records`, mirrored in `twig_record.glsl`): fixed by the plant's pick bits and the twig's number (`pcg3d`, `TWIG_FADE_HASH_USE`), evenly from `twigFadeLeastRank` (the thinning band plus a fade step, so none is left at the last step) to 1.
- **Presence** (`twigFadePresence`): 1 not fading or for the coarser level; else `cardPresence(rank, 1 - levelFadeThreshold(field))`, the field read from the twig's placed entry.
- **Shrinking:** a twig about its base, its prototype position scaled before its matrix (`shadeVertex`); a far leaf about its foot by its own twig's rank (`drawFarCluster`), so a twig and the far leaves made from it go together. Nothing discarded: the prepass keeps testing depth early. Shadow views never fade.
- **Cost:** a few instructions a leaf vertex; the card drawn too for crowns in the band.
- **Not yet:** the climbers and epiphytes still end at the card in one step.
