# Twig drawing

Covers: `src/scene/twig_set`, `src/scene/foliage_shading`, `src/scene/foliage_shared.h`, `assets/shaders/foliage_shading.glsl`, `src/render/gpu/twig_records`, `src/render/gpu/twig_drawing_shared.h`, `src/render/vulkan/vulkan_twig_drawing`, `assets/shaders/twig_drawing.glsl`, `assets/shaders/twig_record.glsl`, `assets/shaders/twig_pass.glsl`, `assets/shaders/twig_sources.comp`, `assets/shaders/twig_cull.comp`

A plant's twigs drawn as instances of a few prototype meshes, culled a brick, then a twig, before their clusters (Nanite's assemblies; `forest-twigs.md` for the forest's).

- **What a game hands over** (`scene/twig_set`, `TwigSet`): the prototypes (drawn-only meshes with given cluster levels), each twig (`TwigInstance`: foot, frame, scale, prototype, lean) and the bricks (`TwigBrick`, `twigBrickSize` 64). `makeTwigSet` splits the feet's box along its longest side at whole bricks until each part is one brick (a median split: compact bricks, all full but the last; a Morton order's runs left one brick 12.6 m across in a 14 m crown), each brick's sphere its farthest twig's foot plus that twig's reach (`meshReach` of its prototype, times its scale).
- **Records** (`render/gpu/twig_records`): `GpuTwig` (64 B: the 3 x 4 matrix from prototype to plant frame, lean, prototype), `GpuTwigBrick` (32 B), `GpuTwigLevel` (32 B a prototype's level: its group's sphere and error, its clusters' run in the pool; `packTwigLevels` refuses clusters that are not a chain).
- **The twig pass's maths** (`twig_drawing.glsl`, mirrored in `render/gpu/twig_records`; 3b-iii-b1): `twigInstance` (the part's instance with the twig's matrix after it, the twig's turn after its normals, its scale on the part's); `twigLevelDrawn` and `twigLevel` (the cluster cut on whole levels: the level whose own error, the level before's group's, fits and whose group's does not, so the twig pass picks exactly the clusters `meshClusterInCut` keeps); `twigBrickKept` and `twigKept` (kept whole where the part is not rigid, as `meshClusterRigid`; else the brick's sphere placed by the part, or the prototype's coarsest group's placed by the twig, in the frustum). `twigDrawsOnCpu` runs the pass's steps for a part (`TwigTables`, `TwigDraw`). Twig entries from `TWIG_INSTANCE_BASE`; a level at most `TWIG_MOST_LEVEL_CLUSTERS` clusters, a prototype at most `TWIG_MOST_LEVELS` levels (`twig_drawing_shared.h`).
- **Twig parts** (3b-iii-b2a): a part carrying a twig set (`RenderPart::twigs`, `DrawItem::twigs`, `CULL_TWIGS`) is drawn only by views drawing twigs (`LevelView::twigs`, `CULL_LEVELS_TWIGS`: the camera drawing mesh clusters with Passes "Twigs" on, off until the twig pass draws), and what it replaces (`ModelRenderer::twigFallback`, `CULL_TWIG_FALLBACK`) only by the others (`cameraTakesTwigs` in `cameraCandidateFlags`); shadows take the fallback either way. In the placed table a twig part's groups draw nothing and its `unused[0]` names its set in `PlacedTable::twigSets`. Its mesh only stands for it (bounds, levels).
- **Drawn** (3b-iii-b2b, `render/vulkan/vulkan_twig_drawing`, Passes "Twigs", on by default: the user, Nov 22 2026): after [PLACED], `twig_sources` (one thread: the camera's placed entries of twig parts, each with its set) and `twig_cull` (a workgroup a source's brick: `twigBrickKept`, `twigKept`, the level walk, an atomic append into the region of the level's cluster count, `GpuTwigEntry`), GPU timer TWIGS. The cluster passes take regions as groups from `TWIG_GROUP_BASE` in the twig parts' style (`PlacedTable::twigStyle`, bindings 18-20); the mesh shaders' `drawnObject` returns the part's instance and the twig's matrix, which `shadeVertex` applies to the vertex first (scene bindings 46-47). Prototype levels come from the pool's records (`GpuMeshCluster::level`). Room grows from the counts read back. `cluster-compare` does not know twigs yet.

- **F4 `TWIGS`** (5-0: where the twig triangles come from): `twig_cull` counts in shared memory and adds once a workgroup (`TWIG_TALLY_*` words after the regions' room, copied back with the counts, `TwigTally`, `readTwigTally`): twigs tested (every twig of every source's bricks), kept, kept for sway (the part not rigid: no frustum test), and each level's twigs and triangles (`GpuTwigLevel::triangleCount`). `twigDrawsOnCpu` adds the same tally. Line: `TWIGS kept/tested  SWAY n  LEVELS a b ...  TRIS aK bK ...`, up to the coarsest level drawn.
- **Leaf look** (3b-iii-c, `scene/foliage_shading`, `foliage_shading.glsl`): in `shadeVertex`, a twig's normal is `blendedFoliageNormal` of its own and `bentFoliageNormal` (the cards' 30% facing plus the twig's lean, never under 0.1 to the facing; the forest's cards use the same function) by Materials "Leaf normal blend" (`MaterialSettings::leafNormalBlend`, 1: the cards' look; `GpuFrameData::leafOptions.x`). Each leaf's tone: a prototype numbers its leaves in the texture coordinate's whole part (`numberedLeafU`; the surface sampler repeats), the vertex shader swaps it for `leafToneSeed(plant pick bits, twig, leaf)` (`tonedLeafU`, 64 seeds: 8 tones 0.85-1.1 x 8 warmths -6..6%, the cards' ranges, `foliage_shared.h`), and a material with `leafTones` (`MATERIAL_LEAF_TONES`) multiplies its colour by `leafToneScale` (the factors on sRGB, to linear by gamma 2.2). A leaf keeps its number at every level, so its tone never pops.
