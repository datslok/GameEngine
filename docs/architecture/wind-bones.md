# Wind bones

Covers: `src/render/gpu/wind_bones`, `src/render/gpu/wind_bones_shared.h`, `assets/shaders/wind_bones.glsl`, `assets/shaders/quat.glsl`, `src/render/gpu/bone_records`, `src/scene/sway_bones_shared.h`, `src/render/gpu/bone_poses`, `src/render/gpu/bone_poses_shared.h`, `assets/shaders/bone_poses.glsl`, `assets/shaders/bone_pass.glsl`, `assets/shaders/bones_claim.comp`, `assets/shaders/bones_pose.comp`, `src/render/vulkan/vulkan_bone_posing`, `src/scene/sway_bones`, `assets/shaders/bone_vertex_data.glsl`, `src/render/gpu/liana_trees`, `src/render/gpu/liana_trees_shared.h`, `assets/shaders/liana_trees.glsl`, `assets/shaders/liana_trees.comp`, `assets/shaders/liana_motion.comp`, `src/render/vulkan/vulkan_liana_trees`, `assets/shaders/bone_cull_data.glsl`

Trees move by bones instead of the per-vertex sway ([wind.md](wind.md), `windSwayOffset`): each frame a pass poses every swaying tree's skeleton on the GPU from the trees' spring at its foot, and the draws move each bound vertex by its two bones' poses. Research and plan: `docs/research/LEAFGEOMETRY.md`, Wind with valid bounds (phase 21 stage 3c). Built: the bones and bindings (3c-i), their motion's maths (3c-ii), every tree mesh bound and the records on the GPU (3c-iii-a), the bones pass (3c-iii-b), bound vertices and twigs drawn by the poses (3c-iii-c-i, ii), lianas' anchors (3c-iii-c-iii), what culling bounds them by (3c-iv-a).

## Bones and bindings

- `SwayBone` (`scene/sway_bones`): pivot, tip, parent (before it in the list, -1 a root), level (0 the trunk, +1 a side chain), the line's radius at its pivot; its chain's first bone, length and radius at its start (`chainStart`, `chainLength`, `chainRadius`).
- `SwayBinding`: a vertex's bone and its weight, the rest its parent's (two-bone blending, GPU Gems 3 ch. 6), so joints never open.
- `appendSwayChain`: a line cut into whole bones of one length near `boneMetres`, each the one before's child; `swayArcOfPoint` (how far along a line a point lies); `swayChainBinding` (the bone at an arc, weight rising from 0 at its pivot over its first `blendShare`, a root's whole); `swayBonesOrdered`.
- Trees: `treeBones` (`games/forest/forest_trees`) cuts each chain, chains taken by first node so parents come first, a side chain's first bone the child of the bone holding its start node; `TreeMeshes::bones`; `bark.swayBindings` (each bark vertex by its own chain's arc), its levels by `simplifyMesh`; the cards whole, each by its node (`appendClusterCards` given the nodes' bindings, `nodeOrNearest`), as `PlacedTwig::binding` (its node's; a filler its nearest node's); climbers and epiphytes by the nearest bark (`swayLines`, `bindToTreeBones`, `swayNearestBinding`); a liana's holds likewise, in the tree's mesh frame (`swayPlantPoint`, `nearLianaShapes`). `ForestTreeSettings::boneMetres` 2 m, `boneBlendShare` 0.5 (F5 Forest, at start-up).

## Motion

(`render/gpu/wind_bones` and `wind_bones.glsl`, mirrored; quaternions in `quat.glsl`, mirroring `math/quat.h`); all in one frame, metres, the tree's lean given in it:

| Function | Does |
|---|---|
| `windBoneShare` | the bone's share of the lean: `2 L / H min((r_foot / r)^k, most)`; an untapered trunk bends as `windSwayOffset`'s parabola |
| `windBranchHertz` | its chain's swing, a cantilever's first mode `0.5596 (r / 2) sqrt(E / rho) / L^2` (`WIND_BONE_FIRST_MODE`), clamped |
| `windBranchPhase` | its chain's phase, `pcg3d(tree seed, chain start)`: a branch's bones swing together |
| `windBoneTurn` | its own turn (rotation vector) from its rest direction: `s (d x W)(1 + a sin t) + s b (W - d (d.W)) cos t`, an ellipse along and across the wind; none in still air |
| `windBonePose` | `T(x) = q x + t` from its parent's, parents first: `T(p_b) = T_parent(p_b)`, so joints never open |
| `windBlendedPlace`, `windBlendedDirection` | a vertex, a normal: its two bones' images blended by its weight |
| `windPosedSphere`, `windSphereUnion` | a cluster's bound: its rest sphere posed by each bone of its run and their parents, merged; no pad |
| `windBoneOwnTurn` | a bone's own turn in its tree (`WindBoneShape`, `WindTreeShape`): its share, its chain's hertz in metres and phase |
| `windTreePoses` | (C++ only) every pose of a tree in list order, for tests and checks |

`WindBoneMotion` (stiffness exponent 3, most 8, wood speed 3000 m/s, branches 0.2-3 Hz, swing along and across 0.3, still at 0.1 of the screen and full from 0.15) is `WindSettings::bones` (F5 Wind, Bones), in the field's record (`GpuWindField::bones`, 336 B).

## The records on the GPU

(`render/gpu/bone_records`, 3c-iii-a)

- A part moved by bones carries its look's `SwaySkeleton` (bones, height in mesh units): `RenderPart::skeleton`, `DrawItem::skeleton`; the forest gives it every swaying tree part (bark, leaves, shadow stand-ins, twigs, climbers, epiphytes), not the impostor card. The placed table lists them once (`PlacedTable::skeletons`), a part's `GpuPlacedPart::unused[1]` its number plus 1.
- `packSwaySkeletons`: `GpuSwayBone` (48 B; parent and chain start within its skeleton), `GpuSwaySkeleton` (48 B: run, height, foot radius, its chain levels, its lean part, reach, rest sphere), `GpuSwayChain` (first bone, count) by level (trunk, its branches, theirs) with each level's start; refuses a chain not a run of children of one level, or starting on a bone not of a lower level.
- Vertex bindings beside the vertex pool, a word a pool vertex (`packSwayBinding`: bone low 16 bits, weight unorm16 high; `SWAY_UNBOUND` otherwise), read by the vertex's place in the pool, so levels with their own vertices and cluster levels need no per-mesh entry; `placeSwayBindings` at upload, the list ending at the last bound mesh (scene binding 48). 4 B a pooled vertex beside its 48 B.
- Twigs: `TwigInstance::binding`, `packTwigBinding`, a word a twig beside the twigs (`VulkanTwigDrawing::getTwigBindings`, scene binding 49).

## The bones pass

(3c-iii-b; `render/gpu/bone_poses` mirroring `bone_poses.glsl`, `render/vulkan/vulkan_bone_posing`, `bones_claim.comp`, `bones_pose.comp`, GPU timer BONES): after [PLACED] and [WIND] (moved there), before the twigs' and cluster passes, while things sway and placed parts carry skeletons.

- **Swaying views:** the camera (placed view 0) and the shadow tiles drawn swaying (`tileSways`); probe captures draw trees still.
- **Claim** (a workgroup a group of a bound part (`boneGroups`) and swaying view, a thread an entry): a plant past the still share of the camera's screen (below) claimed by an `atomicExchange` of the frame's stamp in its word pair (stamp, tree; 8 B a held plant, never cleared after it is made); the claimer takes the next tree (`GpuBoneTree` 32 B, up to `BONE_MOST_TREES` 65536) and its bones' room (`boneTreeFirstPose`: two poses a bone from twice the bones asked before, or `BONE_NO_POSE`, standing still), raising the pose pass's dispatch.
- **Pose** (indirect, a workgroup a tree): its lean now and last frame (`windFadedLean` of the trees' spring at its foot by its skeleton's lean part's bend (`skeletonLeanParts`: its first part swaying as a tree), faded by its screen share (`boneScreenFade`), turned into the plant's frame, `windLeanInPlant`); then its chains a level at a time, a thread a chain bone by bone (`windBoneOwnTurn`: hertz from metres, the plant's scale; seed `boneTreeSeed`, its pick's bits), barriers between levels; `GpuBonePose` (32 B: turn, shift, parent) at first pose + 2 b, last frame's after it.
- **Shared buffers:** header (counts, dispatch), trees, plant table, pool (`WindBoneMotion::poolBones`, 262144 bones, 16 MB, at first; a frame asking for more grows it to the next power of two above the ask, under `poolMostBones`, 4M bones: `boneGrownPool`, read back at the frame's fence, made at the next prepare; a setting changed starts it again); each frame's passes wait for every earlier use. Tables remade (GPU idle first) when the skeletons, lean parts, pool or plants' room change.
- **F4** `BONES TREES n  STILL s  BONES asked/pool`. **`bones-compare`:** the frame's placed lists, trees and pool read back; `boneWantedPlants` against the claims (missing, extra, twice), each pose against `boneTreePoses` (the pass's order, equal to `windTreePoses`) from the leans the GPU wrote.
- **Small on screen, still** (3c-iv-c, as Unreal's `r.Skinning.DefaultAnimationMinScreenSize`): a tree's screen share is its skeleton's rest sphere's diameter over the screen's height from the camera (`boneScreenShare`: radius times the projection's `1 / tan(fovY / 2)` over its centre's distance; `swaySkeletonSphere` about its bones' box's middle, grown by its reach; placed by `boneTreeSphere`). At or below `stillShare` (0.1: a 30 m tree about 150 m off at 90 degrees) it is not claimed and stands at rest; its lean fades in smoothly up to `fullShare` (0.15). The camera decides for every view (`BoneScreen` in the push constants), so a tree and its shadow agree; the plant's sphere is its skeleton's, so its parts agree too. Plants without bones keep the distance fade (`swayFadeStart`, `swayFadeEnd`), as do the lianas' anchor lookup (`boneFootInReach`).
- **Cost grows with:** the claim with the swaying views' bound entries; the pose with the posed trees' bones, twice (large trees on screen, not trees within a reach); memory the pool and 8 B a held plant.

## Drawn by the poses (3c-iii-c-i, ii)

`bone_vertex_data.glsl` (scene set 1: trees 50, each plant's tree 51, the pool 52, the vertices' bindings 48), read by `triangle_vertex.glsl` (the camera's vertex and mesh shaders) and `shadow.vert`, `shadow_cut_out.vert` (tiles that sway):

- **Which vertex:** a placed plant's (`DrawnObject::plant`) pool vertex (`gl_VertexIndex`, a mesh shader's vertex number) bound to a bone, or a twig's (bound by the twig's own binding, scene binding 49).
- **Its tree** (`boneDrawn`): `boneClaimedTree` of the plant's word pair against the frame's stamp in the wind record (`GpuWindField::flags[1]`, `VulkanBonePosing::getNextStamp`, `boneNextStamp`; 0 on a frame the pass does not run, matching nothing), then the tree's first pose and leans.
- **Its poses** (`boneVertexPoses`, mirrored in `render/gpu/bone_poses`): its bone's at `bonePoseSlot` (last frame's after it) and its parent's, named in the pose's fourth word (`GpuBonePose::parent`, -1 a root: the rest pose), so draws need no bone table.
- **Moved** (`boneSwayOfVertex`): the blended place minus the rest, in the mesh's frame, then turned and scaled by the model (bones live in the plant's frame: a bound part sits at its plant's origin, `buildPlacedTable` refuses another); leaves flutter as the sway's (`windFlutterAmount` by the tree's lean); a climber's own anchors move as its bones, its swing as before (`windHangingSway`). Normals and tangents turned by `windBlendedDirection`. The main pass's last place from last frame's poses.
- **Twigs** (ii): each moves whole, by `windRigidPose` at its foot (the twig matrix's place): its two bones' turns blended the short way (nlerp), its foot blended as the bark there is, so it stays on its branch. A mesh shader's workgroup draws one twig's cluster: its first thread makes the pose (`twigPoseOf`, last frame's too in the main pass) and shares it, every vertex then posed by it as by one bone (`drawnPoses`); leaves flutter as the cards' do. Culled by their posed bounds (below).
- **Still:** a bound vertex whose tree is not posed (small on screen, unclaimed, no room, a tile drawn still, a capture) keeps its rest shape; no fallback to the sway.
- **Cost a bound vertex:** a binding word, the plant's pair, a tree record and two poses (four in the main pass); grows with drawn bound vertices. A twig pays that once a cluster, then a pose a vertex.

## Lianas' trees (3c-iii-c-iii)

The CPU hangs the lianas from trees it plants by the same maths as the GPU's cells, so it knows each tree's foot to the bit but not its plant number (packed by the cells' prefix sums). A pass finds it each frame:

- **Records:** `LianaShape::anchors` (128 B record): each hold's binding (`packSwayBinding`, now in `scene/sway_bones`; `SWAY_UNBOUND` for a rope's foot) and its tree's field layer (`LIANA_NO_TREE` none).
- **Tables** (`packLianaTreeTables`, each frame's own): a record a field layer (`GpuLianaTreeLayer` 32 B: slot 0's first plant and count of it, the room a slot, its set's cells' size and table), and a table a set (`GpuLianaCell` 16 B), side² places by column and row modulo the set's side (`plantingSlotSide`), which no two held cells share.
- **Pass** (`liana_trees.comp`, `VulkanLianaTrees`, before the claim in [BONES], a thread an anchor held): the foot's cell by `plantingCellBox`'s edges (`lianaTreeCell`; a float sliver between two cells' boxes holds no plant), its slot, the slot's plants of that layer searched for the foot (`lianaSameTree`, within 1 cm); the plant or `BONE_NO_TREE` a word an anchor (scene binding 53), past the reach none.
- **Drawn** (`lianaAnchorMotion` in `liana_data.glsl`): the hold posed as a bound vertex of that plant (`boneDrawnBy`), its move by `windPlantPointOffset` (the hold in the plant's mesh frame from the plant record, blended, turned and scaled back); still when the tree is not posed. Bones are a tree's only motion: every part that sways as a tree carries the skeleton, every vertex bound (`forest_test`), so none falls to the per-vertex sway, which the palms, understorey, grass and hanging things keep. The lianas do not claim trees: a crown culled from every swaying view while its liana shows leaves that end still.
- **Motions** (`liana_motion.comp`, `VulkanLianaTrees::recordMotions`, after the pose pass, phase 21 4c-vii): a thread an anchor, `lianaAnchorMotion` this frame (none while nothing sways) as a `vec4`, read by the mesh cluster passes (their liana bindings) to bound a liana's clusters; copied back for `cluster-compare`.
- **`bones-compare`:** the frame's found plants and the slots' counts read back with its plants; each held anchor against `lianaTreePlant` (the pass on the CPU): `Lianas: n anchors on trees, w found on the CPU, f on the GPU; m matching, d differing`.
- **Cost grows with:** the held lianas (two threads a record slot, most stopping at the reach test) and, within reach, a cell's trees of the layer; a liana vertex reads two words, two plant records and four poses.

## Bounds for culling (3c-iv)

What the camera's twig and cluster passes bound a posed tree's clusters and twigs by:

- **A cluster's bones** (`meshClusterBones`, `render/gpu/mesh_cluster_culling`): its vertices' own bones, ascending, up to `MESH_CLUSTER_MOST_BONES` 8, 16 bits each in `GpuMeshCluster::bones` (128 B record); `MESH_CLUSTER_MANY_BONES` first when it has more, all `MESH_CLUSTER_NO_BONE` for a mesh without bindings. Packed at upload from the mesh's `swayBindings` (`packMeshClusters`). Its posed sphere: the rest sphere posed by each bone and its parent (named in the pose), merged (`windPosedSphere`, `windSphereUnion`). Bones stay below 65534 (`SWAY_MOST_BONES`) so neither mark is a bone.
- **A skeleton's reach** (`SwaySkeleton::reach`, `GpuSwaySkeleton::reach`, mesh units): the farthest anything bound to it lies from its bones (`swayBindingReach`: from its bone's segment, and its parent's while it blends), over every bound mesh (`swayMeshReach`) and twig (its foot's plus its prototype's reach times its scale; `treeBoneReach`). The forest makes its twig prototypes before its trees for it (`makeTwigPrototypes`).
- **A tree's move** (`GpuBoneTree::move`, this frame, mesh units): the pose pass gathers its bones' largest pivot or tip move E and largest `2 |turn.xyz|` A (`windPoseMoves`, shared-memory `atomicMax` on their bits), then E + A reach (`windTreeMove`; on the CPU `boneTreeMove`). Any vertex blended between a bone and its parent, or twig carried rigidly at its foot, moves no farther; a cluster with too many bones gets its rest sphere grown by it. `bones-compare` checks it (`move` in its line).
- **How a cluster stands** (`MeshClusterPose`, 3c-iv-b; `meshClusterPoseAt`, mirrored in `mesh_cluster_pass.glsl`): **bound** when its vertices are (`meshClusterBound`) or its twig is (its binding not `SWAY_UNBOUND`): never kept whole for the per-vertex sway, since a bound vertex either follows its bones or stands still (an unbound cluster is posed by its sway's reach instead, [mesh-clusters.md](mesh-clusters.md)). **Posed** when its plant's tree is (`boneCullTree`: claimed with the frame's stamp and given room, as the draws find it): a bound mesh's cluster by `meshClusterBonesSphere` (each bone's `windBlendedSphere` with its parent, merged; too many, the rest sphere grown by the move), a twig by its coarsest sphere placed by the twig carried by its rigid pose (`twigPose`, `twigPosed`), a brick by its sphere grown by the move (`twigBrickPose`: a bound part's, `GpuPlacedPart::unused[1]`); each placed, then grown by the leaves' flutter (`meshClusterPosed`, `windFlutterReach`: sqrt(3) |flutter| |lean|).
- **Tested:** the frustum and both occlusion lists by that sphere (`meshClusterCullSphere`); no facing test while posed (blending bends triangles, so the rest cone does not hold). A bound tree not posed stands still: its rest spheres and cone, as a still draw.
- **Read** (`bone_cull_data.glsl`, set 0 from `BONE_CULL_BINDING`): each plant's word pair, the trees, the pool and the twigs' bindings (`BoneCullBuffers`, `BoneCullTables`), and the frame's stamp in each pass's push constants; bindings 21-24 in the cluster passes, 16-19 in the twig passes.
- **`cluster-compare`:** the frame's trees and pool read back with it (as for `bones-compare`), each plant's tree rebuilt from the trees' plants (`bonePlantTreesOf`).
- **Cost:** 8 B more a cluster record; a bone's moves while it is posed and one reduction a tree; a tree's tested cluster up to 16 pose reads (32 B each) and 8 sphere merges, a twig two poses; grows with posed trees' clusters and twigs tested, not with the trees in the forest.
