- `wind-sway` (Nov 16 2026): stage 2 of wind, swaying plants, as planned in `docs/research/WIND.md` and approved by the user.
  - **The sway is on the object, not the material:** each tree is scaled differently, and its climbers and epiphytes must bend against the tree's height to stay on it. `ModelRenderer::sway` (kind, height, bend, flutter) reaches the GPU in a fourth vec4 of the instance (160 to 176 bytes).
  - **No new vertex data:** every mesh stands on its foot with up along y, so height in the mesh, distance from its axis and the world place give the bend, the branches' bob and the flutter's phase. One function of the vertex's place, so bark, leaves and shadows bend alike and nothing tears.
  - **The spring is a lean:** read as radians, softly held under 0.5 so a storm never flattens anything (Horizon's soft clamp, in a simpler form that leaves a breeze unchanged). The tip moves by the lean times its height, a cantilever's h^2 shape below it, pulled back to its length from the foot (Crysis).
  - **The traps from the research:** the velocity pass draws everything swaying within the fade every frame, from last frame's springs and time, so TAA does not smear it; only the sun's nearest cascade sways, redrawn every frame something in it sways, the rest cached and still (Horizon's choice). The sway fades out between 80 and 400 m (first 120 m; the user moved it out, as it looked better from far off): the trees only turn into impostor cards, which stay still, at about 1 km.
  - **A bug from stage 1:** the spring images took turns after the scene set was written, so this frame's and last frame's were bound the wrong way round; invisible until something read them. They now turn in `prepare`.
  - **The user's look:** the trees good; the lower layers "too rippley, like they are just vibrating": the flutter (3 Hz, its phase changing every half metre) was moving grass blades 1.3 cm, near their width, so the plants', grass's and floor's flutter went to a fifth or less (0.04, 0.02, 0.01). The fade's end moved out to 400 m by the user.
  - **Checked:** the tests (release, tests first; the GPU tests' cases written, not run); the debug and release games build without warnings. The user: "i think this is good for now".
- `wind-hanging` (Nov 16 2026): stage 3 of wind, hanging things. The user saw that the lianas did not move with the swaying crowns.
  - **The choice:** a liana's vertex knew neither its crown nor its place along it (the engine's vertex has no room spare), so either every liana bends as a tree standing under it would (no new data, but no real swing: a loop moves least in its middle), or a small stream of hanging data per vertex. The user took the stream (recommended): exact, and the swing grows with how far each point hangs.
  - **Not a vertex attribute:** a storage buffer the shaders read by `gl_VertexIndex` from the mesh's first entry, so no pipeline's vertex input changes and only hanging meshes pay (8 bytes a vertex). Anchors carry the tree itself (foot, height, scale, bend) and the hold, so the shader moves the hold exactly as the tree's own vertex there; the climbers' roots, inside their tree's frame, use the tree's own sway at the vertex instead.
  - **One place for all of it:** every hanging thing is built from `HangingStrand`s through `appendTube` and `appendLeafCards`, so the entries are filled there for the lianas, the islands' roots and vines and the climbers' roots alike; the moss clumps take their root tip's arm.
  - **Checked:** the tests (release, tests first; the GPU tests' cases written, not run); the debug and release games and the GPU tests' program build without warnings (two old missing-initializer warnings in the tests fixed on the way). The user: "im happy with that for now".
- `wind-flutter` (Nov 17 2026): the first wind follow-up, the lower layers' flutter slower and in step (the user, Nov 16: still too rippley after the cut to a fifth).
  - **Why it rippled:** every leaf shivered at 3 Hz, its phase turning once every half metre, so two leaves 25 cm apart on one frond moved opposite ways: a fine ripple across the plant, not a leaf in the wind.
  - **The cure:** a rate per category (`WindSettings::flutters`: hertz, and the metres over which the phase turns once), in the field's record. Leaves closer than about an eighth of those metres shiver nearly together, so 8 m keeps a frond or tuft in step and rolls a slow wave across the meadow; the plants at 1.5 Hz, the grass and floor cover at 1.2 Hz. The trees' leaves and the hanging ivy keep 3 Hz over half a metre, which looked right up high. Live in F5 Wind, each category's group.
  - **One flutter:** the three sines were written out twice (sway and swing); now one `windFlutterOffset`, mirrored, with a GPU case of its own.
  - **Checked:** the tests (release, tests first; the GPU case written, not run); the debug and release games build without warnings. The user: "i think thats good".
- `wind-chain-swing` (Nov 17 2026): the second wind follow-up, longer hanging things swinging slower, as chains do.
  - **The physics:** a hanging chain's slowest swing has the period 5.2255 sqrt(L / g) (2 pi over half the first zero of J0), so 1.7 s for a metre's root and 12 s for a 50 m island vine; one 0.15 Hz spring had swung them all like 15 m lianas.
  - **The choice:** a sine at each strand's own frequency (exact, no field change, but deaf to gusts: it swings the same however the wind blows), or more springs in the field each strand blends (a real answer to each gust; the frequencies between bands blended). The user took the springs (recommended).
  - **As built:** three hanging bands, 0.6, 0.2 and 0.08 Hz (chains of 1.1, 9 and 56 m), so six springs a cell and 12 layers (tiny: 64 x 64 cells); each vertex blends the two bands round its chain's frequency by log frequency, reading only those. The swing's length is the strand's whole length when it hangs from one anchor, its deepest sag between two (a sagging rope swinging about its chord is within about 8% of a chain its sag long), a moss clump's foot's depth; the hanging record grows from 8 to 12 bytes a vertex for it.
  - **Checked:** the tests (release, tests first; the GPU case written, not run); the debug and release games and the GPU tests' program build without warnings. The user: "thats perfect".
- `lod-pop-in` (Nov 17 2026): hiding the switches between mesh levels (the user: "quite bad"; the impostor switches fine).
  - **Research** (`docs/research/LOD.md`, Hiding the switches): Unity, Unreal and CryEngine cross-fade by complementary dithers for about half a second; SpeedTree shrinks a share of the leaf cards away one by one while the rest grow, as Pixar's stochastic simplification (Cook 2007) does; Neubert 2011 found Cook's 1 / share area growth too much for light thinning; Horizon fixed shadow popping with a shadow level per cascade.
  - **Found:** the levels were chosen by the render height, so DLSS Quality pulled every switch a third closer; the shadow tiles already choose one level per cascade (orthographic), Horizon's answer, and following the camera instead would break the shadow cache, so they stay; hysteresis needs per-object memory the per-frame draws do not keep, and a distance-based fade (if step 2 comes) needs none.
  - **As built:** levels chosen by the output height; the trees' leaf cards thinned one by one (`scene/card_thinning`): each card a rank, the share kept the one whose error the view allows (the same pixel budget as the levels), reaching the leaf level's least share exactly where it is chosen; the kept cards first in the mesh, so the level is its prefix, drawn grown by the same records and the swap invisible. Records per vertex in a buffer (binding 42), the draw list's top bit telling the vertex shaders which instances a view draws thinned (a level of its own, like the shadow stand-ins, must not be). `Level card growth` (0.5) for the survivors' growth.
  - **Checked:** the tests (release, tests first; the GPU case written, not run); the debug and release games and the GPU tests' program build without warnings. The user: "looks good". The bark's switch and the step to the shadow stand-ins still swap whole meshes (step 2's dithered fade, if they show).
