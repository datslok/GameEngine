- `vsm-sway-bounds`, second step: clusters swaying without bones culled by their own spheres grown by the sway's reach (`windSwayReach`, `meshClusterSwayingPose`; `passes.meshClusterSwayReach`, on), in the camera's passes and the sun's casters.

  - **The bend's bound is loose:** keeping the length moves a vertex back by at most the sideways step, so 2x; a random bush never needed the scale, the flutter or the trunk distance to stay inside, so each got a case where it rules (a tall plant scaled, a short one fluttering hard, a low branch far out) and each cluster's reach is checked against its vertices' farthest across.
  - **Measured (four spots, atomics, switch on and off): no gain in the pages first.** The swaying draws are grass and small plants already at 0.7 pairs a cluster kept whole, and a cluster takes at least one page, so their 10-23 M were mostly a floor, not waste; the worst lean grew a small plant's cluster past the plant (pages' triangles 1-6% more, camera's at the rim 7% more).

- `vsm-liana-bounds` (Nov 25 2026): 4c-vii, lianas' clusters culled by their spans' bounds as bent (`meshClusterLianaPose`; `passes.meshClusterLianaBounds`, on), in the camera's passes and the sun's casters; the anchors' motions a pass after the bones (`liana_motion.comp`).
  - **The template cut strips, not rings:** in its [0, 1] x [-1, 1]^2 box the tube is short and wide, so meshoptimizer cut strips the whole length (a third of the liana a cluster at best). Stretched to 64 units along (a unit a ring), its clusters are runs of whole rings, an eighth of the liana each.
  - **A cluster's span from its sphere alone:** every tube vertex lies 1 from the axis (an ivy corner √2), so the sphere bounds its x; no new buffer. The ivy's middle cluster still spans the sparse run between the crowns (its cards are few there).
  - **The swing is most of a swaying bound:** 2-3 m still, 5-9 m at the worst lean on a 32 m liana; this frame's lean is left for later, as in 4c-vi.
  - **The stretch broke the draws' spheres:** `boundingSphere` was the half-diagonal times the largest scale, so a liana's grew to hundreds of metres (lianas kept whole 286-692 M of the pages' triangles with the switch off). Now its farthest corner placed, exact for uneven scale (the user's pick, engine-wide).
  - **The driver crashed, silently:** the motion pass read scene bindings declared for vertex and mesh only (nvoglv64.dll access violation at pipeline creation; validation named them); six bindings now name compute.
  - **Measured (four spots, atomics, the switch on and off):** the corner sphere alone cut lianas kept whole to 0.6-15.6 M of the pages' triangles (3.7-42 M on main); the switch on to 0.2-0.9 M. VSMDRAW at the wall 11.0 against 12.9 ms, among the trees 8.4 against 8.8; the rim (6.3) and ring (11.1) even, the camera's passes too (an apparent 0.5 ms on the main pass was drift: alternating off, on, off, on it went). Against main's 4c-iv atomics (rim 7.0, ring 12.0, wall 15.3, trees 11.6 ms) every spot faster. Kept on.
  - **18 planted bugs caught** (the span too tight or its end halved, the arc's radius quartered, the arm without l/2, the ivy's reach or last stray dropped, the template not stretched, the slots' offset, the swing's reach small or its flutter dropped, the anchors' spread or centre wrong, the swing dropped, lianas kept whole, the second anchor misread, no smaller of two, the switch ignored, the sphere by largest scale); GPU tests written, not run.
- `vsm-terrain-bounds` (Nov 25 2026): 4c-viii, terrain patches' clusters culled by their own boxes on the land (`meshClusterTerrainPose`; `passes.meshClusterTerrainBounds`, on), in the camera's passes and the sun's casters.
  - **The bounds on the GPU, not a CPU table:** I first offered a sphere table written each frame for its ease; the user asked what scales and what current engines do. A min/max pyramid a height grid (Far Cry 5's GPU terrain, CDLOD's min/max tree) keeps the CPU out of it and serves GPU selection, terrain occlusion and changing land later. Rule 11 got a line on offering choices.
  - **One range function:** `terrainHeightRange` (the placed plants' cell boxes) now reads four pyramid texels a grid instead of every point in the box; the old loop stays as the test's oracle, which the pyramid's range must hold.
  - **The morph only slides down:** an odd grid point moves to its even neighbour below, so a cluster's box grows one spacing towards the patch's corner only. Forgetting it was caught at once (vertices outside the box near morphing edges).
  - **Footprints as halves:** the clusters' x and z box went into `GpuMeshCluster`'s two unused words, rounded outward (`floatToHalfBelow`, `floatToHalfAbove`); the grids' points (k/32) are exact halves.
  - **Gotchas:** `half` and `patch` are reserved words in GLSL; `sed -i` dropped a file's CRLF even when it changed nothing.
  - **Not timed yet;** the user checked it by eye (looks good). Expected: the terrain's 5.6-7.6 M page triangles about 5 to 10 times fewer.
  - **18 planted bugs caught** (halves rounded to nearest or stepped the wrong way, a footprint from y, packed high rounded down, a pyramid row dropped, a level spanning three texels, the range not one wider, a level's start off, the pyramid's offset before the heights, no morph growth west or north, east rounded in, heights halved, the radius short, terrain still kept whole, the switch ignored, no smaller of two, a patch past the table read); GPU tests written, not run.
- `vsm-soft-shadows` (Nov 25 2026): 4d-i, the sun's soft shadows in its pages by rays over its disc marched through the pool (SMRT, Unreal's; `virtual-shadow-rays.md`): `virtualShadows.rays` 8, `samplesPerRay` 8, `extrapolateSlope` 5.
  - **SMRT, not PCSS:** I first planned the cascades' PCSS for its reuse and an old note ("keeps the approved look"); the user asked why not Unreal's way. Each ray finds its own blocker (layered canopy), and point reads need no page-edge cases. The design's other picks were re-checked too (eviction oldest first owed in 4e, the overflow's level bias owed in 4d-ii).
  - **Thin shells, not a solid heightfield:** Cinnabar counts anything behind the map as solid, which blacks out the point under a leaf smaller than the sun's disc. Here a ray is blocked only by rising through a surface; one hidden by a higher one is carried on behind it (Unreal's extrapolation), or light leaks where blockers overlap.
  - **Unreal's formula is not public:** samples by squared shares (Cinnabar's), the end sample blocking for taller casters (Cinnabar's), the extrapolation's rules ours; the tests hold them to the physics: a plate's edge at 0.25-2 m within 0.04 of the disc's exact share, a leaf leaving 0.84 lit, overlapping plates dark.
  - **Clamped to 12 texels**, the cascades' widest penumbra: a point's samples stay on its 2 x 2 marked pages, so the marking's reach fell from 24 texels to 12.
  - **Not timed yet;** expected about 1 ms at 1440p with 8 x 8 where casters fill the view (Cinnabar: 0.57 ms at 1080p on an RTX 5090).
  - **16 planted bugs caught** (the disc uneven or unturned, shares not squared, the last short of the end, the height past the range, no sun size across, the footprint's side, a solid heightfield, nothing carried on, rising out no hit, ending under not blocked, no bias, the slope ignored or uncapped, the record's rays, too many rays accepted); the first slope bug slipped through until a step test of a rising plate.
